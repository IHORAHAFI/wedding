About StyleHub

Your premier destination for wedding supplies & decorations.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, StyleHub emerged from a passion for creating unforgettable wedding experiences. We recognized that every love story deserves the perfect setting, and every couple deserves access to beautiful, high-quality wedding supplies without the hassle.

What started as a small boutique has grown into Rwanda's leading online marketplace for wedding decorations, supplies, and accessories. Our journey has been guided by the belief that weddings should be as unique as the couples celebrating them.

Today, we serve couples, event planners, and wedding professionals across Rwanda and beyond, offering a curated selection of premium wedding products that transform venues into magical spaces.

Our Mission

To make every wedding day extraordinary by providing exceptional wedding supplies, personalized service, and unforgettable experiences that celebrate love in all its forms.
