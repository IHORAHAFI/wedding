Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to our FAQ page. We want your shopping experience to be great, simple, and transparent. Below is a full explanation of ordering, shipping, returns, and policies.

How do I place an order?

1. Browse products in the Wedding Products catalog.
2. Select the correct quantity, color, and size (if applicable).
3. Click Add to Cart and proceed to checkout.
4. Enter shipping information and select delivery method.
5. Confirm payment and order summary, then click Place Order.

Which payment methods do you accept?

  • Credit card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
  • Debit card
  • PayPal
  • Mobile bank transfer (where supported)

All transactions are protected by SSL encryption.

Where do you ship?

We ship locally and internationally. Estimated transit times are listed in Shipping Policy.

Can I track my shipment?

Yes. After shipping, you receive an email with a tracking number and carrier link.

How does returns and exchanges work?

Please see our Returns & Exchanges page for step-by-step instructions and requirements.

Why was my order cancelled?

Common reasons include: payment authorization failure, item out of stock, or mismatched shipping address. You will receive email notification with a full explanation.

Who should I contact for support?

Email support@weddingdecor.com or use the Contact page form. Include your order number for faster support.
