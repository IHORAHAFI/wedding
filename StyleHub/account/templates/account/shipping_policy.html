Shipping Policy

We want your order delivered quickly, safely, and with full visibility at every step. Please read all steps and requirements before checkout.

Order processing

  • Processing time: 1-3 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding holidays).
  • Orders placed after 3PM may begin processing on the next business day.
  • During high-demand seasons (weddings, holidays), processing may take up to 5 business days.

Shipping methods & timing

  • Standard (5-10 business days)
  • Express (2-5 business days)
  • Overnight (1-2 business days, where available)

Shipping cost

Costs are calculated at checkout by weight, dimensions, destination, and selected method. Free shipping promotions apply when indicated on the product or at checkout.

International shipping

  • Import duties, customs fees, and local taxes are buyer responsibility.
  • Delivery delays caused by customs are outside our control.
  • Some remote regions may incur additional surcharges.

Tracking

Once shipped, you receive email/SMS with tracking number and carrier details. Check your spam folder if you don’t see it in 48 hours.

Address accuracy

Verify your delivery address carefully during checkout. If an order is returned due to wrong address, shipping-reroute & re-delivery costs are billed to customer.

Returns during transit

If your package is returned to us by courier, we will contact you. Re-shipment requires authorization and may incur additional charges.
