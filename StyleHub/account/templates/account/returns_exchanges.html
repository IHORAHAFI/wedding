Returns & Exchanges

We aim to make returns and exchanges clear, fair, and fast. Read every step before submitting your request.

Eligibility

  • Request must be made within 30 calendar days from delivery date.
  • Item must be unworn, unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags.
  • Customized or personalized products may not be eligible unless defective.

Required process

  1. Email support@weddingdecor.com with your order number, item name, and reason.
  2. Wait for our return authorization (RMA number).
  3. Pack item securely with original packing and paperwork. Include original receipt.
  4. Ship via tracked, insured carrier to the address in your authorization email.
  5. Send tracking details to support once shipped.

Refunds

Refunds are issued within 7 business days of receiving and inspecting the item.

Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless we made a shipping error.

Exchanges

To exchange, follow return steps and place a new order for the replacement item. If inventory is low, we can reserve hold by request.

Non-compliance and penalties

  • Returns sent without authorization are refused and returned to sender at customer cost.
  • Items arriving damaged due to poor packaging may be assessed a 15-30% restocking fee or denial.
  • Missing original packaging, tags, or documents may incur a 20% deduction from refund amount.
  • Failure to provide tracking within 5 days may delay refund and incur an additional processing fee of $10.
  • Repeated abuse of returns policy (over 3 non-qualifying returns) can lead to account restrictions.

Need help?

Contact support with all details, photos, and documents, so we can resolve quickly.
